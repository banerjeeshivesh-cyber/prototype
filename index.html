Prototype limitation: this uses a general-purpose object detector, not a custom-trained waste classifier. It cannot reliably detect watches, chargers, loose cables or many other items because those are not among its supported object classes. Bottles, cups and food items are grouped as potential waste—not confirmed waste—and other recognized objects appear as regular items. It can recognize some familiar objects (including phones, laptops, keyboards, mice, TVs and remotes) as possible e-waste, but it cannot reliably tell whether an item is discarded, identify every kind of waste, or detect all electronics such as loose circuit boards and cables. The model may confuse phones with chargers, watches or other small electronics; items labelled “Small electronic item*” are uncertain guesses, not exact identification. Treat alerts as prompts for human review.
